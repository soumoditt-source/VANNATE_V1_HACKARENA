# Vannate Pitch Deck Outline

## Slide 1: Vannate

AI-powered humanitarian intelligence infrastructure. Built in Kolkata, designed for India, made for the world.

## Slide 2: The Belief

A good person gives without being asked. Vannate makes giving trustworthy enough that more people act.

## Slide 3: The Problem

Humanitarian work runs on broken infrastructure: low donor trust, spreadsheets, WhatsApp coordination, slow emergencies, wasted food/resources, language barriers, and fraud.

## Slide 4: The Challenge

The prompt asks how AI can return 10 hours a week to NGOs. Vannate returns 17+ hours by automating reports, grants, donor communication, scheduling, and crisis coordination.

## Slide 5: The Solution

Vannate is not a donation app. It is a humanitarian operating system.

## Slide 6: Six Pillars

Trust Engine, Donation Intelligence, NGO Copilot, Crisis Response, Volunteer Orchestration, Impact Analytics.

## Slide 7: QR Identity

Every donation gets a name. Every name is verifiable. NGO code `108`, donor `10800001`, beneficiary `108-A100000`, package `VN-108-...`.

## Slide 8: Live Demo Story

Donor scans QR, donation registered, NGO accepts, route is tracked, beneficiary confirms, AI writes impact report, trust score updates.

## Slide 9: AI Copilot

RAG-powered assistant for grant writing, impact reports, donor outreach, compliance guidance, volunteer planning, and multilingual voice workflows.

## Slide 10: Disaster Intelligence

Crisis heatmaps, priority needs, volunteer dispatch, shelter capacity, blood-bank coordination, and shortage forecasting.

## Slide 11: Trust and Fraud

VTS 0-1000 score explains government registration, activity consistency, beneficiary confirmations, media proof, reviews, and anomaly flags.

## Slide 12: Resource Redistribution

Restaurants, marriage halls, homes, corporates, pharmacies, and hospitals can list surplus. Vannate routes it to the nearest verified need.

## Slide 13: Why Now

LLMs, open-source voice AI, vector databases, and cheap cloud infrastructure make AI-native NGO automation possible for the first time.

## Slide 14: Market and Rollout

Kolkata MVP, India multilingual scale, global humanitarian infrastructure.

## Slide 15: Business Model

Freemium NGO SaaS, CSR dashboards, transaction rails, premium analytics, government partnerships, white-label humanitarian infrastructure.

## Slide 16: Ask

Mentors, cloud/GPU credits, map/payment/notification credits, and 5-10 Kolkata NGOs for pilot deployment.
