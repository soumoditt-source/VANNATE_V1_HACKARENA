## Product Decisions Derived

- The first screen should be an actual command center, not a generic landing page.
- The product must visibly demonstrate QR identity, route proof, VTS trust scoring, AI copilot, emergency mode, and agentic architecture.
- The app should preserve the deck's visual language: dark navy, cream surfaces, teal trust accents, gold urgency highlights, editorial serif headings, restrained data panels.
- The AI assistant must cite context and collect feedback, because hallucination resistance is central to the concept.
- The demo should be Kolkata-first, with Howrah Sector 4 flood relief as the hero crisis.
